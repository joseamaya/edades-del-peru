# Plaza y templo colonial del Cusco (mission 7)

Scene design for `m7_tupac_amaru`. The playable site is a project-owned low-poly
abstraction of a colonial church and plaza in the Cusco valley. It is **not** a
survey and **not** a historical photograph.

## Sources

- `docs/historia/campanas/tupac_amaru.md`
- `docs/historia/arquitectura.md`
- Colonial chronicles on Cusco's urban order.

## Runtime source

- GLB: `src/assets/sites/colonial/colonial_church_lod0.glb` / `colonial_church_lod1.glb`.
- Source: `art-src/blender/mission-decor/colonial_church_lod0.blend` /
  `colonial_church_lod1.blend` (Git LFS).
- Build/export: `scripts/blender/build_mission_decor.py`.
- LOD1 is the RTS default; `?lod=0` previews LOD0.
- Scale `0.65`, site origin and anchor `(134.8, 73)` on the 192 m map.
- Authored heightfield: `src/model/defs/colonialHeightmap.ts`, generated by
  `scripts/build_colonial_heightmap.py`. The Cusco base, colonial terrace and
  approach are flat at 5 m; the surrounding highland ridges frame the valley.
- A 1 m pathfinding grid, monument blocker and an 8 m walkable corridor connect
  the base to the site. Construction is forbidden over the church footprint.

## Attested elements and abstractions

| Element | Source status | In game |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Colonial church | Documented urban typology | Nave, tower, roof and atrium as a legible abstraction. |
| Plaza / urban order | Documented colonial city | Open plaza around the church. |
| Cusco valley | Documented setting | Authored highland terrain and approach. |
| Exact church plan | Not reproduced | Marked plausible; not presented as a survey. |
