# Plaza y templo colonial del Cusco (mission 7)

Scene design for `m7_tupac_amaru`. The playable site is a project-owned low-poly
abstraction of a colonial church and plaza in the Cusco valley. It is **not** a
survey and **not** a historical photograph.

## Sources

- `docs/historia/campanas/tupac_amaru.md`
- `docs/historia/arquitectura.md`
- Colonial chronicles on Cusco's urban order.

## Runtime source

- GLB: `src/assets/sites/colonial/colonial_church_lod0.glb` / `colonial_church_lod1.glb`.
- Source: `art-src/blender/mission-decor/colonial_church_lod0.blend` /
  `colonial_church_lod1.blend` (Git LFS).
- Build/export: `scripts/blender/build_mission_decor.py`.
- LOD1 is the RTS default; `?lod=0` previews LOD0.
- Scale `0.65 × 256/192`, site origin and anchor `(179.7, 97.3)` on the 256 m map.
- Authored heightfield: `src/model/defs/colonialHeightmap.ts`, generated by
  `scripts/build_colonial_heightmap.py`. The 192 m composition is sampled 4/3
  larger: the Cusco base, colonial terrace and approach are flat at 5 m; the
  surrounding highland ridges frame the valley. `COLONIAL_GROUND` paints the
  valley grass, the terraces dirt and the ridges rock.
- A 1 m pathfinding grid, monument blocker and a 10.7 m walkable corridor connect
  the base to the site. Construction is forbidden over the church footprint.

## Mission acts

The mission follows the documented arc (1780–1781), not a counterfactual capture of Cusco:

1. **Act I · Tinta–Tungasuca.** Micaela Bastidas musters the communities and their supplies at the
   camp, the rebels raise a barracks and the player marches to Tungasuca; the capture of Arriaga is
   narrated, not played.
2. **Act II · Sangarará (18 November 1780).** The column marches to the Sangarará road, where the
   royalists hold the town and its chapel. The player surrounds it and holds the field: it is the
   campaign's documented victory.
3. **Act III · the Cusco siege (January 1781).** The rebels occupy the slopes above the city and hold the
   siege lines. The relief from Lima breaks the siege; the player must withdraw Túpac Amaru to the
   departure point. The city is never taken; the epilogue tells the capture at Langui and the execution
   of 18 May 1781.

The colonial church is the Cusco plaza card; a smaller typological chapel anchors Sangarará on the road.
The map compresses Tinta, Tungasuca, Sangarará and Cusco into one transect; the royalist waves are an
abstraction of the documented actions, not a literal order of battle.

`modelMission: true` are part of the model-mission pass. The colonial GLB was approved on 2026-11-23
(see the quality bar's Art review record); there is no CI pixel gate.
