# Cusco incaico y Quipaipan (mission 6b)

Scene design for `m6b_guerra_civil`. The playable site is a project-owned
low-poly abstraction of an Inca ushnu, plaza and walled precinct at Cusco /
Quipaipan. It is **not** a survey and **not** a historical photograph.

## Sources

- `docs/historia/campanas/guerra_civil_inca.md`
- `docs/historia/civilizaciones/inca.md`
- `docs/historia/arquitectura.md`
- Betanzos, Cieza de León and the Cusco archaeology literature.

## Runtime source

- GLB: `src/assets/sites/cusco/cusco_inca_lod0.glb` / `cusco_inca_lod1.glb`.
- Source: `art-src/blender/mission-decor/cusco_inca_lod0.blend` /
  `cusco_inca_lod1.blend` (Git LFS).
- Build/export: `scripts/blender/build_mission_decor.py`.
- LOD1 is the RTS default; `?lod=0` previews LOD0.
- Scale `0.7`, site origin and anchor `(124.8, 130.6)` on the 192 m map.
- Authored heightfield: `src/model/defs/cuscoHeightmap.ts`, generated by
  `scripts/build_cusco_heightmap.py`. The northern base, Quipaipan terrace and
  approach are flat at 5 m; highland ridges frame the site.
- A 1 m pathfinding grid, monument blocker and an 8 m walkable corridor connect
  the northern base to Quipaipan.

## Attested elements and abstractions

| Element | Source status | In game |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ushnu / ceremonial platform | Documented Inca form | Stepped platform and gold-marked summit. |
| Plaza and walled precinct | Documented Inca urban language | Open plaza, walls and gate. |
| Cusco / Quipaipan setting | Documented campaign | Authored highland terrain and approach. |
| Exact Cusco plan | Not reproduced | Marked plausible; not presented as a survey. |
