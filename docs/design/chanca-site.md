# Pucará chanca de Yahuar Pampa (mission 5)

Scene design for `m5_yahuar_pampa`. The playable site is a project-owned,
low-poly reconstruction of a Chanca fortified height and camp near Cusco. It is
an abstraction of documented highland defensive practice, **not** a survey and
**not** a historical photograph.

## Sources

- `docs/historia/campanas/yahuar_pampa.md`
- `docs/historia/arquitectura.md`
- Betanzos, J. (1551). *Suma y narración de los incas*.
- Pachacuti Yamqui Salcamaygua, J. de S. C. (1613).

## Runtime source

- GLB: `src/assets/sites/chanca/chanca_fortress_lod0.glb` / `chanca_fortress_lod1.glb`.
- Source: `art-src/blender/mission-decor/chanca_fortress_lod0.blend` /
  `chanca_fortress_lod1.blend` (Git LFS).
- Build/export: `scripts/blender/build_mission_decor.py`.
- LOD1 is the RTS default; `?lod=0` previews LOD0.
- Scale `0.7`, site origin and anchor `(112.4, 60.8)` on the 160 m map.
- Authored heightfield: `src/model/defs/chancaHeightmap.ts`, generated by
  `scripts/build_chanca_heightmap.py`. The Cusco base, the Chanca terrace and the
  approach pampa are flat at 5 m; the surrounding highland ridges frame the site.
- A 1 m pathfinding grid, monument blockers and an 8 m walkable corridor keep the
  base connected to the fortress. Construction is forbidden over the site
  footprint.

## Attested elements and abstractions

| Element | Source status | In game |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chanca fortified height / pucará | Defensive practice documented | Platform, walls and three towers as a legible abstraction. |
| Chanca camp | Campaign context | Walled enclosure and gate; no exact camp plan is claimed. |
| Cusco pampa and approach | Documented setting | Authored highland terrain and flat approach corridor. |
| Cusco defenses | Scenario abstraction | Four starting pucarás around the player center keep the siege playable. |
| Exact fortress plan | Not preserved | Marked plausible; not presented as a survey. |

## Status

The site, heightfield, collision, heritage card, real-command victory path and
save/load path are implemented. Current captures are local review artifacts;
there is no CI pixel gate. The model remains a representative low-poly
abstraction, not a measured reconstruction.
