# Chavín model mission (m2 redesign)

Design and status for rebuilding `m2_chavin` to the standard of the Caral model mission
([`model-mission-pattern.md`](./model-mission-pattern.md)). The historical facts and their certainty
live in [`docs/historia/campanas/chavin.md`](../historia/campanas/chavin.md); this document is the
implementation contract.

## Goals

1. **Live the period.** The player arrives at a living ceremonial centre on the Mosna valley terrace,
   not at today's ruins.
2. **One historical problem, one mechanic.** Chavín's power is ceremonial prestige: offerings and
   pilgrimage fund the temple's construction and its long-distance exchange.
3. **End with the documented outcome.** The mission closes with the fragmentation of Chavín's
   influence, framed as history rather than as the player's failure.
4. **No invented history.** No named rulers, no Chavín–Paracas war, no closed chronology.

## Mission structure

Target length: 30–40 minutes. Four acts inside one `MissionDef` (`src/campaign/defs/campaign.ts`).

| Act | Date         | Historical idea                          | Mechanic                                      | Site phase                    |
| --- | ------------ | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| I   | c. 900 a.C.  | The oracle receives pilgrims and gifts   | Reach the site; hand over the first offerings | `old_temple_foundation`       |
| II  | Generations  | The Old Temple and the Lanzón gallery    | Hand over stone; hold the plaza; build fair   | `old_temple`                  |
| III | c. 500 a.C.  | The New Temple and long-distance exchange | Expand Edificio A; research; great pilgrimage | `new_temple_foundation` → `new_temple` |
| IV  | c. 500–200 a.C. | Influence fragments; documented decline | Tour the centre and its record                | `apogee`                      |

- Act objectives start `hidden: true` except Act I and are revealed by triggers.
- Chapter cards (`act` effect) open between acts and pause the mission clock.
- Optional goals: Plaza Circular, gallery, Portada de las Falcónidas, the major offering. They never gate
  victory and are summarised in the epilogue.
- `narrator: 'Voz de los peregrinos'`; no invented speaker.

The briefing subtitle uses the mission window **c. 900–200 a.C.**; the period badge shows the full
**Horizonte Temprano · 1200–200 a.C.** Both are intentional: the mission covers the monumental stretch of
the period, as the dossier explains.

## Map

- 256 m `andes` transect with painted ground every 2 m (128 samples), built by
  `scripts/build_chavin_heightmap.py`; the site kit and heritage proxies moved with the new site origin.
- Sequence: highland ridges → Mosna river → valley floor → ceremonial terrace → foothills.
- The site keeps its authored heightfield, river, fords and ceremonial terrace; the kit draws the temple
  by phases.

## Site phases

`ChavínPhase` and `CHAVIN_PHASE_PLAN` live in `src/model/defs/chavinCity.ts`.

| Phase                   | Stands                                                        |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `old_temple_foundation` | Terraces, Plaza Circular, Edificio B foundation, gallery       |
| `old_temple`            | Finished Edificio B / Templo Viejo                             |
| `new_temple_foundation` | Edificio A foundation                                          |
| `new_temple`            | Finished Edificio A and Portada de las Falcónidas              |
| `apogee`                | Fair tents, pilgrims and the living centre                     |

Heritage cards stand from their phase (`CHAVIN_HERITAGE_SINCE`), and their blockers only block once the
building exists. The hidden source GLB keeps the click proxies; the kit draws the visible city.

## Economy

- `allowedBuildings`: `HOUSE`, `MILL`, `FARM`, `MARKET`.
- `allowedUnits`: `WORKER`, `SCOUT`.
- No military: there is no documented Chavín–Paracas war.
- Technologies are filtered to the match period and civilization; `chavin_pilgrimage` is the mission's
  own research goal.
- The market is the game's abstraction for the pilgrimage fair and is labelled as such in its card.

## Opening, briefing and closing

- Three narrated intro beats with in-engine captures: the valley, the Old Temple and the pilgrimage.
- `MISSION_ART.m2_chavin` registers the mission's own art; the briefing never borrows another mission's
  image.
- Chapter cards use `intro-templo`, `intro-nuevo` and `intro-declive`; the epilogue uses `intro-declive`.
- Captures are generated by `scripts/capture_chavin_intro.mjs` and documented in `PROVENANCE.md` as game
  captures, never historical photographs.

## Tutorial

Nine non-blocking steps: select, site, offering, farm, Old Temple, pilgrimage, fair, New Temple, record.
They advance on what the player does and never repeat the briefing.

## Performance

- `scripts/bench_scene.mjs` includes the Chavín opening and late (apogee) views; `yarn bench:check`
  asserts both.
- Measured after the 256 m resize: opening ~413k triangles / 160 calls; late ~463k / 223 calls, both
  within the 450k / 600k budgets.
