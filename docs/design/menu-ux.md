# Epic onboarding and campaign flow

Player-facing UX for the title movie, main menu, skirmish setup and the La Senda del Sol
campaign. The visual language is Andean codex/epic: obsidian and gold, textile geometry,
large landscape, restrained motion. Age of Empires and 0 A.D. are used as **UX references
only**; no assets or text are copied.

## First launch

1. Title screen with **Entrar** (the click is also the WebAudio gesture).
2. Short saga movie (`SERIES_INTRO`): Caral, the long history and the four campaigns.
   It is skippable with **Saltar** or `Esc`.
3. Main menu. The movie is remembered in `peru-title-intro` and can be replayed from
   **Ver introducción**.

Returning players go straight to the main menu. `?menu=1`, `?play=1`, `?mission=...` and the
other debug entry points bypass the title movie.

## Main menu

Rendered by `src/ui/SeriesMenu.ts` (`renderMainMenu`). Full-screen `media/menu/caral-dawn.webp` backdrop
(the Sacred City at dawn) with a slow drift and a dark left and bottom shade; a gold two-line title over a
stepped Andean band; a vertical action column:

- Continuar partida (when a save exists)
- Campañas
- Escaramuza (only outside the campaign-only release)
- Códice
- Cómo jugar
- Opciones

The **Tu senda** card (bottom right) shows the current campaign, the next mission with its art and
subtitle, the progress over the whole series, the four chapters (current, open, locked) and a direct
"▶ Jugar …" button. The skirmish configuration form lives in its own screen.

## Campaign path

Rendered by `renderCampaignPath`. `Culturas Preincas`, `El Tahuantinsuyo`, `El Virreinato` and
`Independencia y República` are four chapter cards in a row (art or campaign colour, roman numeral, era,
state badge: Disponible, En curso, Completada, Bloqueada). Selecting an open card shows its summary
(`CampaignDef.summary`), its prologue and its missions in order, each with its number, subtitle, a short
blurb from the mission history and a play or replay button.

**A campaign opens with its first mission as soon as it is released** (`isMissionUnlocked` in
`src/campaign/progress.ts`): a fresh profile can start any chapter whose opening exists, and inside that
chapter each mission opens once the previous one is completed. A chapter whose first mission is still
planned waits for the previous chapter, so a planned opening never lets the player skip ahead. Completed
missions stay open to replay. Locked cards and missions are disabled and say what to complete.
`?menu=1&unlock=all` opens everything for testing.

Progress lives in `peru-campaign` as a versioned JSON object. The old numeric value is migrated to the
released mission order (legacy `2` becomes Caral + Chavín); old draft missions are not deleted from the code,
but they do not carry into release progress.

## Mission flow

Campaign path → mission intro (when the mission has one, e.g. Caral's three narrated beats) → mission
briefing → chapter title card → camera flyover → gameplay. The victory screen is an epilogue page with
"Continuar" to the next mission of the campaign.

The **briefing** (`src/ui/MissionBriefing.ts`) is a full-screen chapter page: the mission's own art as the
backdrop (or a plain one lit with the campaign colour, never another mission's image), the story, context,
"¿Sabías que…?", commanders and sources on the left; a sheet on the right with the facts (period, geography,
sides), the visible objectives as a checklist with an "Opcional" tag, the focused "▶ COMENZAR MISIÓN"
button and "← Volver a campañas".

## Asset provenance

`public/media/menu/caral-dawn.webp` is a graded runtime capture of `m1_caral` (regenerated by
`node scripts/capture_caral_intro.mjs --only=menu`). The campaign and mission thumbnails reuse Caral's intro
captures (`CAMPAIGN_ART`, `MISSION_ART` in `src/ui/menuArt.ts`). They are atmospheric game captures,
**not** historical photographs and **not** Blender renders; see `public/media/menu/PROVENANCE.md` and the
root `PROVENANCE.md`.

## Accessibility

- Skip button and `Esc` for every cinematic; `Enter`/`Space` advances.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` removes the backdrop drift and the menu and briefing entrance animations.
- Keyboard focus starts on **Entrar**; in the briefing it starts on **COMENZAR MISIÓN**, so `Enter`
  starts the mission; menu controls and campaign cards are real buttons.
- The background is a static WebP, so the menu does not add a second 3D render.
