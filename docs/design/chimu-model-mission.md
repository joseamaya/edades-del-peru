# La Guerra del Norte model mission

Design and status of `m6_chimu_inca`, the Chimú–Inca mission of La Senda del Sol. It follows the
model-mission pattern ([`model-mission-pattern.md`](./model-mission-pattern.md)) and the campaign quality
bar ([`campaign-quality-bar.md`](./campaign-quality-bar.md)). The historical facts and their certainty live
in [`docs/historia/campanas/chimu_inca.md`](../historia/campanas/chimu_inca.md); this document only
references them.

## The historical problem

After the Collao, Cajamarca and the southern coast, the Inca state still lacked the north coast, held by
Chimor under Minchancaman. The campaign fought at Paramonga, advanced through Huarmey and the Santa valley,
and Túpac Yupanqui **dried the canal** that supplied Chan Chan; the desert city capitulated and Minchancaman
was taken to Cusco. Querrotumi's revolt in 1475 was defeated later. The mission's mechanic is water as
power: advance, close the gate, and receive the surrender.

## Acts

| Act                   | Place           | Player action                                      | Historical idea                                                   |
| --------------------- | --------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I · Paramonga         | North coast     | Advance to Paramonga and keep Túpac Yupanqui alive | The first clash and the curaca reinforcements open the campaign   |
| II · El canal         | Chan Chan canal | Reach the canal and close its gate                 | Water is the city's lifeline and the campaign's lever             |
| III · La capitulación | Chan Chan       | March to the city and hold the surrender for 30 s  | The desert capital capitulates; the north joins the Tahuantinsuyo |

Optional: take Chan Chan's centre if it resists, or hold the camp for ten minutes. The epilogue narrates
Minchancaman's transfer and Querrotumi's 1475 revolt.

## Map

A 256 m transect generated by [`scripts/build_chimu_heightmap.py`](../../scripts/build_chimu_heightmap.py),
sampling the former 192 m composition 4/3 larger:

- **Pacific shore and the Inca camp** (west), the irrigated valley and the canal corridor.
- **Paramonga and the Chimú approach** (centre-east), with the enemy camp and the adobe compound.
- **Chan Chan** (east): the authored `chimu_site`, the capital in the desert.
- **Dunes, ridges and the canal** frame the coastal transect.

Painted ground uses `CHIMU_GROUND` (beach sand, valley grass, terrace dirt, foothills rock) sampled at
128 × 128 over 256 m. The worker crosses in ≈2:22 (`unitSpeedScale: 0.75`), inside the model-mission band.
The horizontal composition scales 4/3 (start offsets, wave centres and radii, canal length and gate radius,
site proxies and corridor included). The heightfield keeps the 192 m vertical amplitude, so slopes read
gentler; the site GLB scales uniformly with the terrace. Both are presentation trade-offs, not a survey.

## Site

`chimu_site` (scale `0.7 × 256/192`, anchor `(190.4, 86.7)`, camera height 73.3) reuses the Chimú compound
GLB, its heritage proxy, blocker and corridor, scaled with the terrace. The site has **no construction
phases**: Chan Chan is a living capital, not a monument the player builds; the exception is recorded here and
in the quality bar.

## Economy and units

- Inca workers, warriors, riflemen and honderos; the Chimú side fields warriors and honderos.
  `UnitKind.MUSKETEER` and `UnitKind.RIFLEMAN` are legacy ranged ids: in this era they render as a hondero
  and an arquero, not firearms.
- The canal gate is Act II's mechanic (`canal_closed`, heritage `chimu_canal`); the water is an abstraction
  of the documented cut, not a reconstructed canal trace.
- The narrator is **Túpac Yupanqui**, a documented commander.

## Opening and closing

- Three narrated intro beats and the three act cards captured in engine by
  [`scripts/capture_chimu_intro.mjs`](../../scripts/capture_chimu_intro.mjs): the north coast, Paramonga, the
  canal and the capitulation, plus a dusk epilogue.
- `MISSION_ART` (`m6_chimu_inca` → `media/chimu/intro-costa.webp`) and the briefing use the coast capture.
- Victory: the canal is closed and Chan Chan holds the surrender; the Act III card uses the capitulation
  capture. The epilogue (`media/chimu/intro-epilogo.webp`) narrates Minchancaman's transfer and
  Querrotumi's revolt. Defeat: the
  Inca column or Túpac Yupanqui is lost.

## Tutorial

The non-blocking steps cover selection, Paramonga, the canal and Chan Chan; the historical context stays in
the briefing, the chapter cards and the Codex.

## Tests and evidence

- Bench: `scripts/bench_scene.mjs` measures the opening (~499k tris / 273 calls) and the surrender
  (~765k / 516, over the 600k target); `yarn bench:check`.

- Save compatibility: `SAVE_VERSION` 10 rejects v9 m6 saves (their 192 m coordinates cannot be carried
  over); `REPLAY_VERSION` 37.

## Pacing

The real-command bot reaches the documented ending in about 4 min of simulated time in the E2E (the harness
marches and holds); a human playtest is still needed to measure the 20–30 min path. The model pattern's
30–40 min band is not claimed.
