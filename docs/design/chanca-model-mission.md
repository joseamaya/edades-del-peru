# Yahuar Pampa model mission

Design and status of `m5_yahuar_pampa`, the Inca–Chanca mission of La Senda del Sol. It follows the
model-mission pattern ([`model-mission-pattern.md`](./model-mission-pattern.md)) and the campaign quality
bar ([`campaign-quality-bar.md`](./campaign-quality-bar.md)). The historical facts and their certainty live
in [`docs/historia/campanas/yahuar_pampa.md`](../historia/campanas/yahuar_pampa.md); this document only
references them.

## The historical problem

In 1438 the Chanca confederation marched on Cusco. Viracocha and Inca Urco fled; Cusi Yupanqui organised
the defence with Cusco's allies and won. The tradition of the pururaucas turns the defence into legend, and
the documented outcome is the Chanca surrender and later integration as military allies, with Cusi taking
power as Pachacútec. The mission's mechanic is the arc: hold the pampa, counterattack the heights, and
close with the pursuit and the accession.

## Acts

| Act                  | Place          | Player action                                                       | Historical idea                                                  |
| -------------------- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I · El asedio        | Yahuar Pampa   | Defend the Cusco centre for 8 minutes against the Chanca waves      | The defence of Cusco is the battle that founds the Tahuantinsuyo |
| II · El contraataque | Chanca heights | March on the pucará and destroy the Chanca camp                     | The counterattack breaks the confederation                       |
| III · Andahuaylas    | Pursuit road   | Pursue the Chanca remnants toward Andahuaylas and close the victory | The victory is followed by surrender, integration and Pachacútec |

The pursuit is the Act III closure. The epilogue narrates the Chanca integration and the start of the empire.

## Map

A 256 m transect generated by [`scripts/build_chanca_heightmap.py`](../../scripts/build_chanca_heightmap.py),
sampling the former **160 m** composition 1.6× larger:

- **Cusco and the pampa** (west): the base, the four fortresses and the defending line.
- **Chanca heights and pucará** (east): the authored `chanca_site`, the enemy camp and the approach pampa.
- **Andahuaylas pursuit point** (far east): the destination that closes Act III. It is a reach point,
  not an authored road.
- **Andean ridges and the dry quebrada** frame the pampa.

Painted ground uses `CHANCA_GROUND` (pampa grass, terrace dirt, quebrada sand, ridge rock) sampled at
128 × 128 over 256 m. The worker crosses in ≈2:22 (`unitSpeedScale: 0.75`), inside the model-mission band.
The horizontal composition scales 1.6 (start offsets, wave centres and radii, pucará proxies and corridor
included). The heightfield keeps the 160 m vertical amplitude, so slopes read gentler; the site GLB scales
uniformly with the terrace. Both are presentation trade-offs, not a survey.

## Site

`chanca_site` (scale `0.7 × 256/160`, anchor `(179.8, 97.3)`, camera height 76.8) reuses the Chanca pucará
GLB, its heritage proxy, blocker and corridor, scaled with the terrace. The site has **no construction
phases**: it is the Chanca camp in use, not a monument the player builds; the exception is recorded here and
in the quality bar.

## Economy and units

- The Chanca host is a marching army: `enemy.economy: false` and `start.enemyOpeningUnits: false`
  keep its camp but skip the peasant economy; a garrison of five warriors and two honderos opens at the
  Chanca centre, and the host keeps the AI's own attacks, which can pull it and the columns toward the
  player's base after their authored move (it targets a building, not the columns). The kill objective is
  contested while the guard still stands.

- Inca workers, warriors and honderos; the Chanca side fields warriors and honderos. No archers, no market
  and no Spanish techs: `UnitKind.MUSKETEER` is the legacy ranged id and in this era it renders as a
  hondero, not a firearm.
- Four fortresses guard the Cusco opening; the enemy is active and aggressive, with scripted waves.
  Each column forms up at its own camp and marches on the Cusco camp, so nothing materialises inside the
  defence; the line no longer claims a compass direction the map does not support.
- The narrator is **Cusi Yupanqui**, a documented figure; the pururaucas are presented as tradition, not as
  an attested event.

## Opening and closing

- Cinema intro (`introStyle: 'cinema'`, as the Tarapacá pilot): Cusco and its pucarás with the Chanca camp
  on the heights (`cine-cusco`, push-in); a campaign map where the two Chanca columns draw themselves from the
  waves' real spawns (`CHANCA_WAVE_NORTH`, `CHANCA_WAVE_EAST`) to `CHANCA_CAMP_STAND`; the Cusco line facing
  the first column on the pampa (`cine-defensa`, pan). It states the siege, not the counterattack or
  Pachacútec. Regenerate with `node scripts/capture_cinema.mjs --mission=m5_yahuar_pampa [--only=cine-defensa]`.
- The three act cards captured in engine by
  [`scripts/capture_chanca_intro.mjs`](../../scripts/capture_chanca_intro.mjs): Cusco and the pampa, the
  Chanca pucará and the Andahuaylas road, plus a dusk epilogue.
- `MISSION_ART` (`m5_yahuar_pampa` → `media/generated/campaigns/la-senda-del-sol/m5_yahuar/intro.webp`) uses the approved
  EEVEE render from `art-src/blender/chanca/chanca_intro.blend`; the in-engine captures remain the act-card and
  epilogue evidence.
- Victory: the pampa holds and the Chanca camp falls. The epilogue (`media/chanca/intro-epilogo.webp`)
  narrates the integration and Pachacútec. Defeat: Cusco falls or Cusi dies.

## Tutorial

The existing non-blocking steps cover selection, the defence, the counterattack and Cusi; the historical
context stays in the briefing, the chapter cards and the Codex.

## Tests and evidence

- Data: `tests/campaign.test.ts`, `tests/campaign-layout.test.ts`, `tests/authored-sites.test.ts`.
- Scale: `tests/campaign-scale.test.ts` enforces 256 m, 128² painted ground and the crossing band.
- E2E: `campaign-playtest.spec.ts` (real orders, no cheats), `campaign-completable.spec.ts`,
  `save-campaign.spec.ts` (later missions) and `hud-layout.spec.ts`.
- Bench: `scripts/bench_scene.mjs` measures the opening (~682k tris / 362 calls, over the 600k target) and
  the counterattack (~761k / 521); `yarn bench:check`.
- Evidence: `yarn record --mission=m5_yahuar_pampa --mode=clip` and the intro/epilogue captures.
- Save compatibility: `SAVE_VERSION` 9 rejects v8 m5 saves (their 160 m coordinates cannot be carried
  over); `REPLAY_VERSION` 36.

## Pacing

The real-command bot holds the full eight-minute siege and reaches the ending in about 41 s of wall-clock
simulation in the E2E; a human playtest is still needed to measure the 20–30 min path. The model pattern's
30–40 min band is not claimed.
