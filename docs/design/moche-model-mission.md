# La Frontera de los Valles model mission

Design and status of `m3_moche_recuay`, the Moche–Recuay mission of La Senda del Sol. It follows the
model-mission pattern ([`model-mission-pattern.md`](./model-mission-pattern.md)) and the campaign quality
bar ([`campaign-quality-bar.md`](./campaign-quality-bar.md)). The historical facts and their certainty live
in [`docs/historia/campanas/moche_recuay.md`](../historia/campanas/moche_recuay.md); this document only
references them.

## The historical problem

Moche and Recuay were contemporaries. The sources attest long-distance interaction with an armed component
(fortifications, elite exchange, Moche-affiliated pieces in the highlands), not a single named battle, and
no Moche or Recuay ruler is known. The mission's mechanic is therefore the valley itself: the canal that
feeds the fields and the huacas, the plaza that must hold against highland incursions, and the elite
exchange that keeps the frontier open.

## Acts

| Act                       | Place                     | Player action                                                     | Historical idea                                                       |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I · El valle (c. 300–800) | Moche valley and canal    | Open the canal gate; the fields and huacas recover                | Water is the valley's power and the canal is its infrastructure       |
| II · La frontera          | Huacas plaza              | Hold the plaza 90 s against Recuay waves                          | The conflict is attested, but as frontier pressure, not one battle    |
| III · El intercambio      | Huaca de la Luna          | Deliver 250 silver in the elite exchange (400 optional)           | Moche and Recuay kept long-distance elite exchange through the frontier|

Optional: complete 400 silver (elite exchange). The epilogue narrates the Moche fragmentation toward 800 AD.

## Map

A 256 m transect generated by [`scripts/build_moche_heightmap.py`](../../scripts/build_moche_heightmap.py),
sampling the former 192 m composition 4/3 larger:

- **Pacific shore and beach** (west), the irrigated **Moche valley** and the player's terrace.
- **Huacas del Sol y de la Luna** (centre-east): the authored `moche_site` with its plaza and corridor.
- **Dry quebrada** and the **Andean foothills** (east), where the Recuay camp and the Pashash decor stand.

Painted ground uses `MOCHE_GROUND` (beach sand, valley grass and dirt, huacas terrace dirt, foothills rock)
sampled at 128 × 128 over 256 m. The worker crosses in ≈2:22 (`unitSpeedScale: 0.75`), inside the
model-mission band. The horizontal composition scales 4/3 (canal length, hold and wave radii, start offsets
and decor included). The heightfield keeps the 192 m vertical amplitude, so slopes read gentler; the site GLB
scales uniformly with the terrace, so the huacas read 4/3 taller. Both are presentation trade-offs, not a
survey.

## Site

`moche_site` (scale `0.25 × 256/192`, anchor `(164, 108)`, camera height 90.7) reuses the Huacas del Sol and
de la Luna GLB, its heritage proxies, blockers, corridor and construction keepout, scaled with the terrace.
The site has **no construction phases**: the huacas are a living ceremonial centre, not a monument the player
builds; the exception is recorded here and in the quality bar.

## Economy and units

- Moche workers, warriors and hondero (ranged) units; the Recuay side fields warrior and hondero waves.
  `UnitKind.MUSKETEER` is the project's legacy ranged id: at this period it renders as a hondero with a
  sling, not a firearm.
- The canal gate is the Act I mechanic (`canal_open`, heritage `moche_canal`); it irrigates nearby farms.
- Recuay has its own camp in the sierra (`layout.enemyBase`, under the authored «Sierra recuay» label).
  Without it the AI camp was placed at random and could land beside the huacas.
- The three columns answer the player's acts instead of fixed minutes: the first enters when the canal
  opens, the other two when the huacas hold and the exchange opens, and all three enter from the sierra
  (`spawn`) to the plaza instead of materialising on it. An idle player is no longer raided at 80 s by a wave
  that was scheduled before the canal was even touched.
- No firearms, horses or engines: the period roster and the Moche/Recuay architecture only.
- The narrator is an anonymous **Voz del valle**: the dossier records that no Moche or Recuay ruler is known,
  so the mission invents no name or title.

## Opening and closing

- Three narrated intro beats and the three act cards captured in engine by
  [`scripts/capture_moche_intro.mjs`](../../scripts/capture_moche_intro.mjs): the valley and canal, the
  huacas terrace and the Recuay foothills, plus a dusk epilogue.
- `MISSION_ART` (`m3_moche_recuay` → `media/moche/intro-valle.webp`) and the briefing use the valley capture.
- Victory: the elite exchange completes; the epilogue (`media/moche/intro-epilogo.webp`) narrates the
  fragmentation toward 800 AD. Defeat: the valley base or the plaza is lost.

## Tutorial

Four non-blocking steps: select the workers, open the canal, hold the huacas plaza and exchange silver at the
Huaca de la Luna. The historical context stays in the briefing, the chapter cards and the Codex.

## Tests and evidence

- Data: `tests/campaign.test.ts`, `tests/campaign-layout.test.ts`, `tests/authored-sites.test.ts`.
- Scale: `tests/campaign-scale.test.ts` enforces 256 m, 128² painted ground and the crossing band.

  `campaign-completable.spec.ts`, `campaign-playtest.spec.ts` (real orders, no cheats) and
  `save-campaign.spec.ts` (canal save/load).
- Bench: `scripts/bench_scene.mjs` measures the opening (~550k tris / 332 calls) and the huacas plaza under pressure
  (~721k / 568, over the 600k art-direction target as m7/m9); `yarn bench:check`.
- Evidence: `yarn record --mission=m3_moche_recuay --mode=clip` and the intro/epilogue captures.
- Save compatibility: `SAVE_VERSION` 7 rejects v6 m3 saves (their 192 m coordinates cannot be carried
  over); `REPLAY_VERSION` 34.

## Pacing

The real-command bot reaches the documented ending in about 5 min of simulated time; a human playtest is
still needed to measure the 20–30 min path. The model pattern's 30–40 min band is not claimed.
