# Conjunto chimú de Chan Chan (mission 6)

Scene design for `m6_chimu_inca`. The playable site is a project-owned low-poly
abstraction of a Chimú adobe compound and the Chan Chan political landscape. It
is **not** a survey and **not** a historical photograph.

## Sources

- `docs/historia/campanas/chimu_inca.md`
- `docs/historia/arquitectura.md`
- Colonial chronicles and public archaeology of Chan Chan.

## Runtime source

- GLB: `src/assets/sites/chimu/chimu_compound_lod0.glb` / `chimu_compound_lod1.glb`.
- Source: `art-src/blender/mission-decor/chimu_compound_lod0.blend` /
  `chimu_compound_lod1.blend` (Git LFS).
- Build/export: `scripts/blender/build_mission_decor.py`.
- LOD1 is the RTS default; `?lod=0` previews LOD0.
- Scale `0.7`, site origin and anchor `(142.8, 65)` on the 192 m map.
- Authored heightfield: `src/model/defs/chimuHeightmap.ts`, generated by
  `scripts/build_chimu_heightmap.py`. The coastal valley, player base and Chimú
  terrace are flat at 5 m; the canal corridor stays low and readable.
- A 1 m pathfinding grid, monument blockers and an 8 m walkable corridor connect
  the base to the site. Construction is forbidden over the compound footprint.

## Attested elements and abstractions

| Element | Source status | In game |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chimú adobe compound | Documented architectural tradition | Walled compound, audience and rooms as a legible abstraction. |
| Chan Chan | Documented capital | Represented by the compound and the enemy center; no single ciudadela is claimed. |
| Canal and water control | Documented campaign mechanic | Operable canal and a reach objective for Túpac Yupanqui. |
| Exact compound plan | Not reproduced | Marked plausible; not presented as a survey. |

## Implementation

- Mission data: `src/campaign/defs/campaign.ts`.
- Site registry: `src/model/defs/sites.ts`; loader: `src/rendering/siteModels.ts`.
- Heightfield: `scripts/build_chimu_heightmap.py` → `src/model/defs/chimuHeightmap.ts`.
- Heritage: `chimu_compound` in `src/model/defs/heritage.ts`.
- Tests: `tests/e2e/campaign.spec.ts`, `tests/authored-sites.test.ts`,

## Status

The site, heightfield, collision, heritage card, canal victory path and save/load
path are implemented. Current captures are local review artifacts; there is no CI
pixel gate. The model remains a representative low-poly abstraction.
