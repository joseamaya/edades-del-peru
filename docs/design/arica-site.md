# Terraza portuaria y batería de Arica (mission 9)

Scene design for `m9_guerra_pacifico`. The playable site is a project-owned
low-poly abstraction of Arica's port terrace, battery and warehouse. It is
**not** a survey and **not** a historical photograph.

## Sources

- `docs/historia/campanas/guerra_del_pacifico.md`
- `docs/historia/arquitectura.md`
- Marina de Guerra del Perú, Combate de Angamos.
- BBC Mundo (2022), Batalla de Angamos.

## Runtime source

- GLB: `src/assets/sites/arica/arica_battery_lod0.glb` / `arica_battery_lod1.glb`.
- Source: `art-src/blender/mission-decor/arica_battery_lod0.blend` /
  `arica_battery_lod1.blend` (Git LFS).
- Build/export: `scripts/blender/build_mission_decor.py`.
- LOD1 is the RTS default; `?lod=0` previews LOD0.
- Scale `0.8`, site origin and anchor `(134.8, 73)` on the 192 m map.
- Authored heightfield: `src/model/defs/aricaHeightmap.ts`, generated by
  `scripts/build_arica_heightmap.py`. The port terrace and player base are flat at
  4 m; the Pacific shore and low coastal ridge frame the site.
- A 1 m pathfinding grid, monument blocker and an 8 m walkable corridor connect
  the base to the port.

## Attested elements and abstractions

| Element | Source status | In game |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Port terrace | Documented setting | Flat authored terrace above the shore. |
| Battery | Documented defensive infrastructure | Low stone battery with gun positions. |
| Warehouse and pier | Documented port functions | Low warehouse and wooden pier. |
| Exact battery plan | Not reproduced | Marked plausible; not presented as a survey. |
| Naval campaign | Documented context | Not simulated; the mission is the land defence of Arica. |

## Implementation
