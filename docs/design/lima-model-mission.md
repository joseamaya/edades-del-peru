# La Ciudad de los Reyes model mission

Design and status of `m_lima`, the opening mission of **El Virreinato**. It follows the model-mission
pattern ([`model-mission-pattern.md`](./model-mission-pattern.md)) and the campaign quality bar
([`campaign-quality-bar.md`](./campaign-quality-bar.md)). The historical facts and certainty live in
[`docs/historia/campanas/lima.md`](../historia/campanas/lima.md).

## The problem

Lima is founded in 1535 on the Rímac, in a valley already inhabited and with Andean allies. In 1536 Quizu
Yupanqui's army descends from the hills to destroy the city. The mission's mechanic is **found and defend**:
trace the plaza and cabildo, keep the curacas, hold the plain against the siege and break it by taking
Quizu's camp. The epilogue does not hide the rest: Cusco is still under siege.

## Acts

| Act | Place | Player action | Historical idea |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I · La traza (1535) | Lima plain | Visit the plaza, build the cabildo and the plaza | The city is a political foundation, not a lone fort |
| II · El cerco (ago 1536) | Lima | Hold the plaza for 90 s and keep Pizarro alive; optional curacas | The relief columns to Cusco are lost; Lima must resist |
| III · La ruptura | Lima and the hills | Take Quizu's camp | The cavalry and the allies break the siege in the plain |

Victory = the camp falls and the siege lifts. Optional: the curaca pact.

## Map

A 256 m coast transect generated by [`scripts/build_lima_heightmap.py`](../../scripts/build_lima_heightmap.py):

- **Mar del Sur** (west): sea and beach.
- **Rímac** (centre): an east-west channel with a land bridge on the campaign route; the city is on its
  south bank.
- **Llano de Lima**: the flat plain where the city is founded.
- **Cerros de Quizu** (north-east): the enemy camp and the descent route.

The heightfield is 129² over 256 m (2 m per sample), the painted ground 128². The worker crosses in ≈2:22
(`unitSpeedScale: 0.75`).

## Site and factions

`lima_site` reuses the colonial plaza/church GLB on the new transect; the plaza card (`lima_plaza`)
represents the traza, cabildo and first church. The city is a typological abstraction.

- **Hueste indiana**: a new VICEROYALTY civilization (pizarristas and allies), with rodeleros, arcabuces
  escasos, jinetes and light artillery. It shares the realista outfit kit.
- **Inca de Quizu**: the Inca civilization, with the historical `minPeriod` units of the conquest.

## Economy

Workers, rodeleros, arcabuces, cavalry, artillery, a missionary and a scout. Buildings: town economy,
barracks, arsenal, stable, artillery workshop, fortress, walls, gate, tower and market. No modern units.

## Opening and closing

- Three narrated beats: the founding, the siege, the plain.
- Two chapter cards (Acts II and III).
- Victory: the camp falls, Quizu dies and the siege lifts; the epilogue states that Cusco remains besieged.
- Defeat: Lima falls.

## Tutorial

Six non-blocking steps: select infantry, trace the plaza, build the cabildo, learn the cavalry, hold the
siege, take the camp.
