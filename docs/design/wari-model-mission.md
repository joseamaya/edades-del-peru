# El Horizonte Medio model mission

Design and status of `m4_wari`, the Wari–Tiahuanaco mission of La Senda del Sol. It follows the
model-mission pattern ([`model-mission-pattern.md`](./model-mission-pattern.md)) and the campaign quality
bar ([`campaign-quality-bar.md`](./campaign-quality-bar.md)). The historical facts and their certainty live
in [`docs/historia/campanas/wari_tiahuanaco.md`](../historia/campanas/wari_tiahuanaco.md); this document only
references them.

## The historical problem

Wari and Tiahuanaco were two coeval supra-regional states that spread the staff-god cult. No single
Wari–Tiahuanaco war is documented and no ruler is known, so the mission's weapon is the state itself: the
network of provincial centres and roads, the andenes that feed it, and presence on the frontier. The
competition is measured in influence, not in an invented battle.

## Acts

| Act                      | Place                      | Player action                                                   | Historical idea                                                          |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| I · Los andenes          | Ayacucho basin             | Gather 600 food and condition two andenes                       | Agriculture and stored surplus fund the state                            |
| II · La red              | Core and provincial nodes  | Build a market at two provincial nodes and connect them         | Planned centres and roads articulate the first Andean empire             |
| III · La frontera        | Frontier node              | Hold the frontier node 60 s; the 8-minute endurance is optional | Coetaneity and influence, not a documented war, define the rivalry       |

Optional: extend the network with a third provincial centre, or sustain the sphere for eight minutes. The
epilogue narrates the collapse of both states toward 1000–1100 AD.

## Map

A 256 m transect generated by [`scripts/build_wari_heightmap.py`](../../scripts/build_wari_heightmap.py),
sampling the former 192 m composition 4/3 larger:

- **Ayacucho basin and the player's terrace** (west): andenes, fields and the base.
- **Wari complex** (centre): the Wari kit's D-shaped temple, plaza, galleries and mural.
- **Provincial nodes A and B** (east) and the frontier terrace, linked by authored roads.
- **Highland ridges and dry quebradas** frame the basin and the provincial route.

Painted ground uses `WARI_GROUND` (basin grass, terrace dirt, quebrada sand, ridge rock) sampled at 128 × 128
over 256 m. The worker crosses in ≈2:22 (`unitSpeedScale: 0.75`), inside the model-mission band. The
horizontal composition scales 4/3 (node offsets, radii, roads and their widths included). The heightfield
keeps the 192 m vertical amplitude, so slopes read gentler. The kit pieces are placed in world metres on the
flat site pad. Both are presentation trade-offs, not a survey.

## Site

`wari_site` (anchor `(143.3, 136)`, camera height 93.3) draws the complex from the shared Wari kit
([`wari-chanca-sites.md`](./wari-chanca-sites.md)); its heritage proxies, blockers, corridor and construction
keepout follow the same layout. The site has
**no construction phases**: it is the operating capital, not a monument the player builds; the exception is
recorded here and in the quality bar.

## Economy and units

- Wari workers, warriors and the Wari guardian; no arsenal, stable, pucará, walls or towers in the roster,
  and no ranged units. The institute and the smelter are the state workshops («Talleres estatales»,
  «Taller de orfebrería») that host the loom and quipu techs.
- No enemy is active on the map: the mission is an administrative contest, and the Tiahuanaco influence is
  measured by the frontier objective, not by waves.
- No firearms, horses or engines; the narrator is an anonymous **Voz de los Andes** because no Wari or
  Tiahuanaco ruler is known.
- `startingEra: TAHUANTINSUYO` is the **internal tech level** that `HistoricalPeriod.MIDDLE_HORIZON` maps to
  (`getPeriod(...).level`), not a claim that the mission is Inca; no UI surface prints that internal name —
  the top bar and every card show «Horizonte Medio · 600–1000 d.C.» (`hud-labels.spec.ts`), the architecture
  uses the Wari override (fieldstone, thatch, planned compounds) and the Wari Guardian's `eraStats` stay
  keyed to that same era. `tests/defs.test.ts` enforces the mapping. The gold band on the base is Wari's own
  civ palette (`secondary: '#d8b06a'`), not the Inca era; changing it is an art decision about Wari identity,
  not a data fix.

## Opening and closing

- Cinema intro (`introStyle: 'cinema'`, see the Tarapacá pilot): the Wari complex (`cine-complejo`,
  push-in); a campaign map where the roads from Ayacucho to the junction and the two provincial nodes, and
  the dashed optional third centre, draw themselves from the mission's constants (`WARI_BASE`,
  `WARI_JUNCTION`, `WARI_NODE_A`, `WARI_NODE_B`, `WARI_BONUS_NODE`); Tiahuanaco is the frontier, a place,
  not an army; a crew on the road toward the complex (`cine-camino`, pan). It does not tell the outcome.
  Stills: `node scripts/capture_cinema.mjs --mission=m4_wari [--only=cine-camino]`.
- The three act cards captured in engine by
  [`scripts/capture_wari_intro.mjs`](../../scripts/capture_wari_intro.mjs): the Ayacucho basin, the Wari
  complex and the highland frontier, plus a dusk epilogue.
- `MISSION_ART` (`m4_wari` → `media/wari/intro-ayacucho.webp`) and the briefing use the basin capture.
- Victory: the network and the frontier hold; the epilogue (`media/wari/intro-epilogo.webp`) narrates the
  collapse toward 1000–1100 AD. Defeat: the base or the network is lost.

## Tutorial

The existing non-blocking steps cover selection, andenes, the two-node network and the frontier hold; the
historical context stays in the briefing, the chapter cards and the Codex.

  `REPLAY_VERSION` 35.

## Pacing

The real-command bot builds both farms and the two provincial centres with ordinary gather/build orders and
reaches the documented ending inside the E2E's 620 s simulation cap. The harness no longer builds the network
instantly; a human playtest is still needed to measure the 20–30 min path. The model pattern's 30–40 min band
is not claimed, and the eight-minute survival is optional rather than a gate.
