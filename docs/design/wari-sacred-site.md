# Complejo Wari (mission 4)

Scene and mission design for `m4_wari`. The playable site is a project-owned,
RTS-scale synthesis of Wari's monumental core: a D-shaped temple, walled plaza,
galleries and terraces. It is a representative reconstruction, **not** a survey
and **not** a historical photograph.

## Runtime source

- Architecture kit: [`wari-chanca-sites.md`](./wari-chanca-sites.md) (`src/assets/architecture/wari/`,
  nine pieces, built by `scripts/blender/build_wari_architecture_kit.py`).
- Layout and blockers: `WARI_CITY_LAYOUT` / `wariCityBlockers()` in `src/model/defs/wariChancaCity.ts`;
  the site sets `hideModel` and `simulationCollision`.
- The retired `wari_site` GLB (`src/assets/sites/wari/`, `scripts/blender/build_wari_site.py`) stays in the
  registry and is no longer drawn.
- LOD1 is the RTS default; `?lod=0` previews LOD0.
- The authored heightfield (`src/model/defs/wariHeightmap.ts`, generated by `scripts/build_wari_heightmap.py`)
  owns the ground: highland basin, terraces and the two provincial-node plateaus at 5 m. The dry quebrada
  now stops at the site edge, so the complex keeps one flat pad.
- The site keeps the player's 1 m grid; the kit layout keeps the south road open.
- Visual road ribbons connect the base, the site and both provincial nodes
  (`MissionRoad`). They follow the terrain, have no collision and do not affect
  pathfinding.

## Deliberate abstractions

- The site combines documented Wari forms without claiming a specific building
  inventory or exact dimensions.
- The `Mercado` building represents a provincial administrative/exchange node,
  not an attested market form.
- The 600-food, two-farm, two-centre and 60/480-second targets are pacing
  abstractions. The 300 wood / 100 silver opening stock is a scenario balance
  so two provincial markets can be founded without a long prelude; the 600-food
  target still forces agricultural expansion.
- There is no active enemy: the competition with Tiahuanaco is expressed through
  influence, presence and dialogue, not an invented war.

## Mission flow

1. Gather 600 food and prepare two andenes.
2. Found two provincial centres at the network nodes; the roads show the
   intended network but are visual only.
3. Hold the first frontier node for 60 seconds.
4. Sustain the Wari sphere for eight minutes.
5. Optional: found a third centre.
