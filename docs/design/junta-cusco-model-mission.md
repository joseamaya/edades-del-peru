# La Junta del Cusco model mission

Design and status of `m_junta_cusco`, the first mission of **La Independencia**. It follows the model-mission
pattern ([`model-mission-pattern.md`](./model-mission-pattern.md)) and the campaign quality bar
([`campaign-quality-bar.md`](./campaign-quality-bar.md)). The historical facts and their certainty live in
[`docs/historia/campanas/junta_cusco.md`](../historia/campanas/junta_cusco.md); this document only references
them.

## The historical problem

In 1814 the Cusco junta rose against the Real Audiencia and sent columns across the south Andes. It won at La
Apacheta and occupied Arequipa, but a veteran royalist division under Ramírez destroyed it at Umachiri in
March 1815. The mission's mechanic is the Junta's real dilemma: **many people, few firearms, and a race
against a veteran column**. The player can win a tactical delay, not the war; the epilogue tells the
documented defeat.

## Acts

| Act | Place | Player action | Historical idea |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I · La Junta (3 ago 1814) | Cusco | Visit the Cabildo and raise a barracks | The junta is a urban political coalition; it must organise before marching |
| II · La Apacheta (10 nov 1814) | Arequipa pass | March south and hold the pass against Picoaga's vanguard | A tactical victory opens Arequipa but does not stop Ramírez |
| III · Umachiri (11 mar 1815) | Puno altiplano | Reach the plain and hold for 90 s; keep Pumacahua alive | Numbers without firearms cannot break a veteran division; the leaders must survive |

Optional goals: protect Melgar and take the royalist headquarters if it holds. Neither gates victory.

## Map

A 256 m transect generated by [`scripts/build_junta_heightmap.py`](../../scripts/build_junta_heightmap.py):

- **Cusco basin** (north-west): the player base, the colonial plaza and its cabildo card, terraced fields.
- **La Apacheta** (centre): a pass between the basin and the altiplano, named and painted as a battlefield.
- **Río Lalli and Umachiri** (south-east): the altiplano plain, the river and its crossing.
- **Royalist base** (south-east edge): the column that descends from the Alto Perú.

Painted ground uses the authored `JUNTA_GROUND` weights (grass, dirt, sand, rock) sampled at 128 × 128 over
256 m. The worker crosses in ≈2:22 (`unitSpeedScale: 0.75`), inside the model-mission band.

## Site

`cusco_junta_site` reuses the existing colonial plaza/temple GLB (`colonial_church`) on the new heightfield.
The temple is the Junta's Cabildo card: an `inspect` objective with a radius, so the player must bring a unit
to the plaza and open the card. The rest of the city is represented by the authored site, the painted basin
and the colonial architecture of the period; no new Blender kit was required for this first pass.

## Economy and units

The match is locked to `HistoricalPeriod.INDEPENDENCE` and `Era.REPUBLIC`:

- Workers, militia (`soldier`), musketeers, scarce riflemen, montoneras, cavalry/hussars, artillery, scouts
  and a missionary. No tanks, aircraft, modern infantry or nuclear weapons.
- Buildings: the town economy plus barracks, arsenal, stable, artillery workshop, fortress, walls and a
  market. No institute or wonder.
- The montonera is the mission's light unit: it flanks and harasses, but does not hold a line. The tutorial
  says so explicitly.

## Opening and closing

- Three narrated intro beats: the Junta, the four columns, Umachiri.
- Two chapter cards (Act II and Act III) with the documented dates.
- Victory: the royalist column is delayed and the leaders can withdraw; the epilogue states that the
  rebellion was defeated, Pumacahua was executed in Sicuani, the Angulo brothers and Béjar in Cusco, and
  Melgar was shot.
- Defeat: Pumacahua dies or the base falls.

## Tutorial

Six non-blocking steps: select infantry, visit the Cabildo, raise the barracks, learn how to use montoneras,
hold La Apacheta, resist at Umachiri. The informational montonera step advances on its own.

## Tests and evidence

- Data: `tests/junta-cusco.test.ts` checks the acts, hidden objectives, chapter cards, scale and roster.
- Scale: `tests/campaign-scale.test.ts` enforces the 256 m map, 128² ground and crossing band.
- E2E: the general campaign specs cover briefing, epilogue, HUD layout, save/load and the campaign path;
  the mission-specific playtest reaches the acts with real orders.
- Bench: the opening view is measured by `scripts/bench_scene.mjs`; it is not a passing scene yet (see open item 3).
