# Capilla y campamento patriota de Ayacucho (mission 8)

Scene design for `m8_independencia`. The playable site is a project-owned
low-poly abstraction of a campaign chapel and patriot camp on the Junín–Ayacucho
route. It is **not** a survey and **not** a historical photograph.

## Sources

- `docs/historia/campanas/independencia.md`
- `docs/historia/arquitectura.md`
- Basadre, J. *Historia de la República del Perú*.

## Runtime source

- GLB: `src/assets/sites/independence/independence_chapel_lod0.glb` /
  `independence_chapel_lod1.glb`.
- Source: `art-src/blender/mission-decor/independence_chapel_lod0.blend` /
  `independence_chapel_lod1.blend` (Git LFS).
- Build/export: `scripts/blender/build_mission_decor.py`.
- LOD1 is the RTS default; `?lod=0` previews LOD0.
- Scale `0.65`, site origin and anchor `(134.8, 71)` on the 192 m map.
- Authored heightfield: `src/model/defs/independenceHeightmap.ts`, generated by
  `scripts/build_independence_heightmap.py`. The Junín base, Ayacucho terrace and
  approach are flat at 5 m; highland ridges frame the pampas.
- A 1 m pathfinding grid, monument blocker and an 8 m walkable corridor connect
  the base to the site.

## Attested elements and abstractions

| Element | Source status | In game |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign chapel | Documented religious architecture in the Andes | Nave, tower and roof as a legible abstraction. |
| Patriot camp | Campaign context | Tents and low camp wall; no exact camp plan is claimed. |
| Junín / Ayacucho pampas | Documented battles | Two flat highland zones connected by the route. |
| Exact chapel or camp | Not reproduced | Marked plausible; not presented as a survey. |
