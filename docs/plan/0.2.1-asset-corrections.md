# 0.2.1 — asset corrections plan

**Status:** Approved · **Created:** 2026-11-20 · **Source:** [`release/0.2.0-asset-historical-audit.md`](../release/0.2.0-asset-historical-audit.md) ·
**Scope:** three P0 and the P1 site/label corrections from the 0.2.0 asset audit. No new missions, no catalogue
redesign, no campaign scope change.

**Implementation:** A1–A3 landed in #560, B1–B4 in #561 and B5–B9 in #562 (2026-11-20).

This plan sequences the findings of the 0.2.0 asset audit. The audit is a **snapshot**: it records what was
wrong at `7dd3907` and does not get edited when a finding is fixed. This document owns the remediation and
links back to the finding ids (`P0-1` … `P1-9`). The P2/P3 list stays in the backlog; it is not scheduled here.

## 1. Why

The audit found three P0 that the player sees in the first minutes of a campaign:

- **P0-1:** the Republican era builds glass, steel and LED strips (`buildModern`) in 1814–1883.
- **P0-2:** the Viceroyalty era builds concrete with a radar-like dome, antenna and electric windows
  (`buildConcrete`/`buildMilitary`) in 1535–1780.
- **P0-3:** the Inca of 1536 and 1572 use the 1780 `rebelde_*` outfit and carry a musket.

The first two contradict `docs/historia/arquitectura.md` (adobe/quincha with tile; neoclassical/port). The
third contradicts the mission dossiers and the 1532 cutoff for Inca equipment. All three are visual-data
problems: no new campaign, no new mechanic, no save format change.

## 2. Scope

**In scope**

- Remap the `Era.VICEROYALTY` and `Era.REPUBLIC` building models to an existing, period-appropriate family.
- Make the Inca outfit depend on the mission (1536/1572 vs 1780).
- Fix the site identity and the labels of the P1 findings.
- Add regression tests and fresh captures for every change.

**Out of scope**

- The 12 P2 and 9 P3 findings (blockout detail, shared Andean kit, colonial housing variety, friezes, props).
- New missions, new factions, new mechanics, saves, URL params or `localStorage` keys.
- A catalogue redesign or a new art direction.
- The unverified list in audit §10 (Blender inspection, exact site plans); it is a separate verification task.

## 3. Workstream A — P0 first

`eraModel[Era.REPUBLIC]` entry that points at `*_atomic`, `*_information`, `plant_*`, `exchange_*` or
`*_modern` with the existing stone/brick family below. The `*_imperial` types already exist in
`buildStructure` and are used by the Andean regional states; for a non-Andean civ they call `buildCivic`,
`buildHouse` (`house_steam`), `buildBarracks`, `buildStable`, `buildFactory`, `buildFortress`,
`buildMarket` and `buildDropsite` (mill/sawmill/smelter) with a stone or brick wall and a tile/flat roof. No
new model is required.

`CONTEMPORARY` is untouched: it keeps `plant_*` and `buildModern`. `refinery` is the only type without an
imperial twin. Change `case 'refinery'` in `ProceduralModel.ts` from
`buildConcrete(b, 2.2, false, true)` to `buildCivic(b, 2.2, 'stone')`; `buildCivic` already branches to
`buildAndeanCivic` for the Andean eras, so the Tiahuanaco/Inca quarry keeps its platform and the colonial
quarry becomes a stone shed. That removes the radar dome without a new model.

**Cleanup after the remap:** `buildConcrete(..., radar)` and `buildMilitary(..., antenna)` are no longer
reached by a campaign era. Remove the `radar`/`antenna` parameters and the emissive window strips from those
two functions, or assert in a test that no campaign era maps to them. Keep the functions for the
`CONTEMPORARY` models (`buildModern`) and for future use.

**Acceptance**

- No `eraModel[Era.VICEROYALTY]` or `eraModel[Era.REPUBLIC]` entry resolves to `*_atomic`, `*_information`,
  `plant_*`, `exchange_*` or `*_modern` (except `farm`/`wonder`, which are already correct).
- A unit test asserts the mapping and the absence of `radar`/`antenna` in those eras.
- Fresh captures of `m_lima` (1535), `m7_tupac_amaru` (1780), `m_junta_cusco` (1814) and `m9_guerra_pacifico`
  (1880) show brick/stone/teja, with no glass façade, LED strip or dome. Attach them to the PR.
- Costs, HP, armor, build times and footprints do not change (visual-only mapping).

### A2. Inca outfit by mission (P0-3)

**Change:** `unitModelAsset` currently decides `rebelde_*` from `civ === INCA && era === VICEROYALTY`; that
catches 1536 and 1572 as well as 1780. Add the mission to the build context:

1. `BuildContext` (`src/model/ProceduralModel.ts`) gains `missionId?: string`.
2. `Game.makeBuildContext()` sets `missionId: this.mission?.def.id ?? this.settings.missionId` (the method
   that already builds `era`, `civ` and `arch`; `RuntimeController.ctxFor` returns `UnitContext`, a different
   type).
3. `UnitView` passes `ctx.missionId` to `unitModelAsset` (new optional argument).
4. `unitModelAsset` returns `rebelde_*` only when `missionId === 'm7_tupac_amaru'`. For
   `m_cusco_1536` / `m_vilcabamba` it must **force the Andean lookup**: `andeanCultureForEra(INCA,
   Era.VICEROYALTY)` is `null` (the civ def only lists `LATE_HORIZON`), so call it with `Era.TAHUANTINSUYO`
   (or add an explicit `andeanCultureForMission`) to get `inca_soldier_*` / `inca_ranged_*`. The mission's
   `HistoricalPeriod` and stats are unchanged; only the rig lookup is forced.
5. `m_cusco_1536` and `m_vilcabamba` keep the Inca weapons: spear/macana and sling; no musket.

**Acceptance**

- `m_cusco_1536` and `m_vilcabamba` resolve every Inca humanoid to `inca_*`; `m7_tupac_amaru` resolves to
  `rebelde_*`.
- The unit test covers the three missions plus a skirmish (no mission → no `rebelde`).
- A capture of each mission shows Inca soldiers without a musket in 1536/1572 and the earth-tone rebel with
  musket in 1780.

### A3. Regression test

Add one focused test file (or extend `tests/model-assets.test.ts` if it exists) that:

- iterates `BUILDING_DEFS` and asserts the era mapping above;
- asserts `unitModelAsset(SOLDIER, VICEROYALTY, INCA, …, 'm_cusco_1536')` is not `rebelde_*` and the m7 case is;
- runs in `yarn test` (no browser).

## 6. Release handling

- **If 0.2.0 ships before A1–A2:** the release notes must state that the late-era architecture and the Inca
  outfit of 1536–1572 are pending abstractions. The 0.2.0 notes now carry that line.
- **0.2.1** closes A1–A3 and B1–B9, including the realistas era split inside P1-9 (morion 1535–1572 vs
  bicorn 1780–1826).
- No save migration, URL or `localStorage` change is expected. The model choice is presentation-only.

## 7. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| The `*_imperial` family reads too "early modern" for 1880 | Review the four captures; if needed, add a `republican` family as a follow-up, not in this pass. |
| `house_steam` is a legacy name but a house shape | The name is internal; the visual is what matters. Do not rename the type in this pass. |
| `unitModelAsset` signature change ripples | Add an optional parameter with a default; update **four** call sites (`UnitView`, the two recursive calls and `modelAssetIdsForMatch`, which preloads the rigs) and test. |
| P0-2 cleanup deletes a model used by a mod | Keep the `*_atomic` types in `buildStructure`; only the campaign mapping changes. |
| Site art expands | Orders 5–9 are procedural, mission-scoped compositions with no new GLB, so they cost draw calls, not bundle size. |

## 8. Definition of done

- A1–A3 merged with green `yarn verify:fast` and a unit test that fails on the old mapping.
- Fresh captures for the P0 missions and every P1 site, attached to their PRs.
- The release notes (0.2.0 or 0.2.1) state what remains.
- The backlog rows B18, B19 and the site half of B15–B17 move to Done as their scope closes; the audit
  snapshot is not edited.
