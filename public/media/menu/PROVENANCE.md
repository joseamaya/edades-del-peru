# Menu backdrop provenance

`caral-dawn.webp` is a UI-tuned capture of the running game's Caral scene. It is used only as
the title and menu backdrop; it is **not** a historical photograph, a campaign illustration or a
reconstruction claim.

- Source: runtime capture of `?play=1&mission=m1_caral` with the Sacred City in its "in use" phase
  and a few workers at the circular plaza, HUD hidden, 1920×1080, a low camera from the south-west at
  dawn. It is regenerated by `node scripts/capture_caral_intro.mjs --only=menu`.
- Transformation: a darker top edge, a soft vignette and +8% saturation. The map's west edge shows at
  the top left, under the menu's own left shade.
- No terrain, building or historical detail was added or removed. The in-game site remains
  the source of truth; this image is presentation only.

- Output SHA-256: `f082f0ee8a951401eefc667738fb541d02041214fe8cac349be829eeb2d4baa0`

The campaign and next-mission thumbnails in the menu reuse the m1 intro captures
(`public/media/caral/intro-*.webp`, documented in the root `PROVENANCE.md`).
