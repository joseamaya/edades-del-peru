# Menu backdrop provenance

`caral-dawn.webp` is a UI-tuned capture of the running game's Caral scene. It is used only as
the title/menu backdrop for the Caral onboarding; it is **not** a historical photograph, a
campaign illustration or a reconstruction claim.

- Source: runtime capture of `?play=1&mission=m1_caral`, HUD hidden, 1920×1080.
- Transformation: warm split-tone grade, vignette, soft bloom and light film grain.
- No terrain, building or historical detail was added or removed. The in-game site remains
  the source of truth; this image is presentation only.
